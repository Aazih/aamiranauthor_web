# Canada under Mark Carney

Canada, under the Liberal Prime Minister Mark Carney, has become a debased and humiliated nation here in 2025. It gives me no joy in saying this as I voted for my local Liberal candidate Adam van Koverden. Even knocked doors for him. He's been a massive disappointment ever since as well.

## Debased

I say debased because on the 21st of November Canada signed a massive investment deal with the UAE[^1] less than a month after the UAE funded RSF carried out a massacre so horrific in El Fasher in Sudan that it could be seen from space[^2].

So at a time when global consciousness finally recognized Emirati complicity in Sudanese massacres and the need to turn the UAE into a pariah, Mark Carney does the exact opposite. A depraved act.

## Humiliated

I say humiliated because of the many many ways Canada has been slapped in the face by the Zionist state of Israel and just accepted it. The latest is when Canadian MPs
were attempting to cross into Occupied Palestine from Jordan and one, Iqra Khalid was pushed over and over by an Israeli guard saying "I'll touch you as much as I want"[^3].

The only response to this humiliation thus far has been a meek raising of objections by the Minister of Foreign affairs[^3].

Global affairs Canada instead of raising hell has done nothing and when questioned by journalist Rachel Gilmore issued a craven response where they made sure to make
clear the delegation was not representing the government of Canada.

What does that matter? An Israeli border guard assaulted a Canadian member of parliament. Where exactly are our elbows?
